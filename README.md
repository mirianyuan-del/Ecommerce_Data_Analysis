# Ecommerce Data Analysis | E-commerce User Behavior and Business Analysis

> This project uses synthetic e-commerce data generated by ChatGPT and applies Python, Pandas, and Jupyter Notebook to analyze customer behavior, orders, products, regions, fulfillment, and reviews.  
> **The data is synthetic and does not represent the results of a real business.**

## Project Overview

This project addresses the following e-commerce business questions:

- How do GMV, order volume, and customer scale change over time?
- Do customers make repeat purchases?
- Which customers may be at risk of churn?
- Which products contribute the most revenue and estimated gross profit?
- Which regions perform better?
- What issues can be identified in delivery performance and customer reviews?

## Analysis Workflow

```text
Order, customer, payment, product, and review data
                      ↓
Filter completed orders with status = delivered
                      ↓
Aggregate payment records by order_id
                      ↓
Build a core behavior table with one row per order
                      ↓
Active users, GMV, repeat purchases, retention, churn, and LTV
                      ↓
Product gross profit, regional performance, delivery, reviews, and payment preferences
```

## Core Analytical Definitions

### Order Scope

Only completed orders with the status `delivered` are included in the analysis.

### Payment Scope

A single order may contain multiple payment records. Therefore, payment amounts are first aggregated by `order_id` and then joined with the order table to avoid double-counting GMV.

### Churn Definition

The day after the final purchase date in the dataset is used as the snapshot date:

```text
No purchase for more than 90 days = churned customer
```

### Product Profit Definition

```text
Net product revenue = Product price × (1 - Discount rate)

Estimated product gross profit = Net product revenue - Product cost
```

Estimated product gross profit does not include platform commissions, warehousing, fulfillment, marketing, or taxes. Therefore, it should not be interpreted as net business profit.

## Analytical Limitations

- The data was generated by ChatGPT and does not represent a real market.
- The 90-day churn rule is a simple business rule, not a complete churn prediction model.
- Product gross profit does not include all operating costs.
- The dataset does not contain `channel`, `source`, or `campaign` fields, so customer acquisition ROI cannot be analyzed.
- Regional, delivery, product, and review findings are primarily descriptive; correlation does not imply causation.
